# Non-Technical Report: Sangam — Smart AI Government Scheme Platform

**Project Name:** Sangam (Connecting Citizens to Government Welfare & Financial Benefits)  
**Target Audience:** Non-technical stakeholders, decision makers, government officials, general citizens, and management.

---

## 1. What is Sangam? (Project Vision & Purpose)

Every year, the Central and State Governments of India announce thousands of financial welfare schemes, agricultural subsidies, low-interest business loans, and student scholarships worth hundreds of thousands of crores.

However, millions of eligible citizens miss out on these benefits because:
1. Information is scattered across hundreds of different government portals.
2. Official guidelines are written in complex legal language that is difficult to understand.
3. Citizens do not know which specific schemes apply to their exact age, income, caste, or occupation.

**Sangam** solves this critical problem. It acts as a **smart digital bridge** that automatically matches every citizen to the government benefits they qualify for, guides them through required documents, and lets them ask questions in plain natural language.

## 2. How Sangam Works: Simple Step-by-Step Flow

```
[ Step 1: Create Profile ] ──> [ Step 2: Instant Smart Match ] ──> [ Step 3: Chat with Sarthi ] ──> [ Step 4: Apply with Ease ]
  Fill basic details:            Our AI analyzes 850+ schemes        Ask questions like "Am I        Get exact document list
  Age, Income, Caste,            and ranks them with a match        eligible for student loan?"     and direct official link.
  Location & Profession.         score (e.g. 95% Match).             Get clear, instant answers.
```

### Step 1: Citizen Profile Setup
Citizens sign up and provide basic, non-sensitive demographic details such as:
- Age / Date of Birth
- Annual Household Income Bracket
- Caste / Social Category (General, OBC, SC, ST)
- Gender & Occupation (e.g., Farmer, Student, Entrepreneur, Self-Employed)
- Location (State, District, Village)

### Step 2: Automated Smart Matching
Instead of forcing citizens to scroll through endless PDFs, Sangam’s intelligent matching system checks their profile parameters against active government rules in milliseconds. It shows a personalized list of schemes along with a **Match Score (e.g., 95% Match)** and a clear 1-sentence reason explaining why it fits them.

### Step 3: Sarthi — Your AI Assistant
Citizens can click on the **Sarthi AI Chatbot** icon anytime to chat in plain language.
- *Example Query:* "I am a female farmer from Gujarat earning under 2 Lakhs. What subsidies can I get for drip irrigation?"
- *Sarthi's Response:* Checks live database records and gives direct, accurate recommendations with eligibility requirements.

### Step 4: Step-by-Step Application Guidance
For every matched scheme, Sangam provides:
- Exact monetary benefit (e.g., ₹60,000 scholarship or 3% loan interest subvention)
- Clear list of required documents (Aadhar, Income Certificate, Bank Passbook, etc.)
- Direct link to the official government application portal.

## 4. Why Sangam's AI is Different & Trustworthy

Generic AI chatbots often "hallucinate" (make up fake rules or non-existent schemes). **Sangam's AI is engineered differently:**

1. **Strictly Grounded in Real Data:** Sarthi is programmed to only answer using verified schemes currently stored in our official government database. If a scheme does not exist, it explicitly states so.
2. **Zero Fake Information:** Every recommendation includes actual ministry names, real monetary figures, and official website links.
3. **Daily Automated Refresh:** A background automated system checks government portals every night to ensure deadlines, amounts, and rules stay 100% up to date.

## 5. Privacy, Security & Control

- **Citizen Data Privacy:** Profile information is encrypted and securely stored. Data is used solely for eligibility matching.
- **100% Free Access:** Sangam is completely free for all citizens without hidden fees.
- **Instant Control Room:** Platform administrators have a live "Feature Control Panel" to enable or disable sections (e.g., temporary maintenance on loans or chatbot) with a single click without taking down the website.

## 6. Social & Economic Impact Summary

Sangam empowers citizens by putting government welfare directly into their hands. By simplifying bureaucracy, eliminating confusion, and making scheme discovery as simple as a Google search, Sangam ensures that no eligible Indian citizen is left behind.
